# Reports and what discounts cost

## What you see

**Reports** cover the last 30 days.

- **Free**: the number of orders, how many had a discount, **what the discounts
  cost** (the money they took off) and the **average order**, plus a chart of the
  cost day by day.
- **Pro**: the same **per discount** — how many orders it was in, what it cost and
  the revenue of those orders — and the number of gifts given and sale items sold.
  On Free this part shows a labelled example.

Quantity discounts are one row together, free gifts another. A discount that was
renamed or deleted since, and discounts that are not Won's, are counted under
**Other discounts in the orders**.

## What the numbers mean

**Cost** is what the discount took off. **Revenue** is the subtotal of the orders
the discount was part of. The report does not claim what you would have sold
without the discount — it gives you the two numbers to judge.

Cancelled orders are left out. Amounts are in your store's currency.

## Where the numbers come from

Each new order is recorded once, without the customer's name, e-mail, address or
id — only the order's number, its totals and its discounts. Orders are counted from
the moment the app is allowed to read them; older orders are not added. Records are
kept for 400 days.

## No numbers yet

Shopify has to approve the app's access to orders. Until it does, Reports say so
and stay empty — nothing is estimated.
