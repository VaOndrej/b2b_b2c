# Prepare for uninstalling

After an uninstall the app has no access to your store and **cannot put anything
back**. Do this first — it works on any plan.

1. Open **Settings**, scroll to **Plan** and find **Prepare for uninstalling** (it is
   shown only when there is something to put back). It lists what it will do:
   how many running clearance sales it ends (their prices go back to what they were
   before the sale) and which Shopify discounts it restores (the ones you moved into
   Won with **Move**).
2. Click **Prepare for uninstalling**.
3. Read the result. *Done* with the counts means you can uninstall. If part of it
   failed, the page says what; click again before you uninstall.

What it does **not** do: it does not delete your Won discounts or settings. Shopify
removes the app's discounts together with the app, and the settings are kept for 48
hours in case you reinstall.

If nothing is listed (no running sale, no moved discount), there is nothing to put
back and you can uninstall straight away.
