# Move Shopify discounts into Won

1. Open the **Overview**. **Discounts outside Won** lists the Shopify discounts that
   run next to Won. (Setup offers the same in its second step.)
2. Click **Move** next to one discount, or **Move all**.
3. Read the dialog. It says what happens, **what you lose** (for example the usage
   count) and what to keep in mind for this discount.
4. Confirm. The moved discounts appear under **Moved to Won**, each with Undo.

## After the move

- If the discount had a fixed amount or a minimum, it now exists in the shop currency
  only. Open it in **Discounts & codes** and add the other currencies, or it is not
  offered there.
- Check a typical cart in [try-a-cart](try-a-cart).
- Discounts that cannot be moved stay in the list with the reason. Won does not count
  them; decide whether to keep them in Shopify.

## If a move did not finish

The Overview shows it under unfinished moves. Click **Undo** there; the discount
comes back from the backup.
