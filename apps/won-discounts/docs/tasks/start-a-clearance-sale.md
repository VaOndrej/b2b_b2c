# Start a clearance sale

1. Open **Discounts → Clearance**.
2. In **New sale**, click **Pick a variant** and choose the product and its variant.
3. Enter **Pieces to sell** (the quota) and **Discount (%)**.
4. Optionally pick an **End** date. The sale ends at the start of that day in your
   store's time zone.
5. Tick the market **price lists** whose fixed price should get the discount too.
6. Click **Start the sale**.

## Notes

- One sale per variant at a time; up to the store limit in
  [../reference/limits.generated.md](../reference/limits.generated.md).
- A ticked price list without a fixed price for this variant is left as it is;
  the result says how many.
- How it works: [../concepts/clearance.md](../concepts/clearance.md).
