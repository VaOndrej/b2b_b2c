# Schedule a campaign

1. Open **Discounts → Campaigns** (Pro).
2. Under **New campaign** enter a **name**.
3. Set the **start** and the **end**: a date and a time (HH:MM) each. Times are in
   your store's time zone; the screen says which one and what time it is there now.
4. Under **What the campaign changes**, tick each discount the campaign changes and
   choose:
   - **During the campaign**: *Unchanged*, *Switch on* or *Switch off*;
   - a **new value** (a percent, or an amount per currency) — empty keeps the
     discount's own value. Free shipping can only be switched on or off.
5. Under **Quantity discounts in the campaign**, tick a set to give it other breaks
   during the campaign. The rows start with the set's usual breaks: raise the
   values, or add a break in an empty row. Every quantity must get the same or a
   higher discount than usual; the form says which quantity does not.
6. Click **Schedule campaign**. It is saved and sent to Shopify at once.
7. In the campaign's card click **Try a cart during the campaign** and run a cart:
   the result is what checkout gives at the campaign's start.

## A discount only for the campaign

Under **A new discount only for this campaign** in the same step:

- tick **Add a discount off the whole order that runs only during the campaign** and
  enter the percent (for example 30 % off everything on Black Friday), or
- click **Create another discount for the campaign** for a code, an amount or a
  discount on some products only. The discount editor opens; after saving you are
  back in the campaign, what you typed is kept and the new discount is ticked.

Either way the discount is saved in Discounts and codes switched off. The campaign
switches it on at its start and off again at its end.

## Timing

The table on the product page shows the campaign's breaks from a minute after the
start until 7 minutes before the end; checkout gives them the whole time.

To stop a running campaign at once, click **End now** in its card. A scheduled one
can be cancelled the same way or deleted.
