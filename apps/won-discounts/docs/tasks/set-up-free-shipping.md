# Set up free shipping from an amount

1. Open **Discounts → Milestones**.
2. Click **Add a step** (the first time: **Add the first step**) and under
   **What the customer gets** choose **Free shipping**.
3. In the table **From what cart value**, enter the amount for each of your
   markets (for example `1 000` for Czechia and `40` for Slovakia). Amounts are
   in the currency's normal units. **Suggest the other markets** fills the
   empty ones from the rate you set for the market in Shopify.
4. Save.

Free shipping can be one step only.

## Notes

- The amount counts the items in the cart before discounts; the free gift does
  not count. See [../concepts/cart-rewards.md](../concepts/cart-rewards.md).
- A market left empty gets no free shipping; the screen names the market. See
  [../support/free-shipping-missing-in-a-market.md](../support/free-shipping-missing-in-a-market.md).
- Whether free shipping adds up with your other discounts follows the
  combination switches in Settings: see
  [switch-combination-categories](switch-combination-categories).
- The cart on your store shows the ladder ("X more and you get: Free
  shipping") only with the app embed on.
