# Set up free shipping from an amount

1. Open **Cart rewards**.
2. In **Free shipping**, switch on **Offer free shipping from an amount**.
3. Enter the amount for each currency of your markets (for example
   `1 000` in CZK and `40` in EUR). Amounts are in the currency's normal units.
4. Save.

## Notes

- The amount counts the items in the cart before discounts; the free gift does
  not count. See [../concepts/cart-rewards.md](../concepts/cart-rewards.md).
- A currency left empty gets no free shipping; the screen names the market. See
  [../support/free-shipping-missing-in-a-market.md](../support/free-shipping-missing-in-a-market.md).
- Whether free shipping adds up with your other discounts follows the
  combination switches in Settings: see
  [switch-combination-categories](switch-combination-categories).
- The cart on your store shows "X more for free shipping" only with the app
  embed on.
