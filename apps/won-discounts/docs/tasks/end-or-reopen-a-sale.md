# End or reopen a clearance sale

## End a running sale

1. Open **Discounts → Clearance**, find the sale under **Running sales**.
2. Click **End the sale**. The prices come back and the sale mark is removed.

## Pieces returned after the end

When an order of an ended sale is cancelled or refunded with restock, the
default setting asks you on the **Overview** and on the sale itself:

- **Open again** starts the sale again with the pieces left of its quota. The
  current prices are backed up anew first.
- **Keep it ended** leaves everything as it is.

Choose the behaviour in **Clearance → Display and returns → A return after the sale
ended**. On Free a sale is never opened again.
