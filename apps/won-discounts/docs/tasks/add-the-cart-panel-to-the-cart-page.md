# Show the rewards in the cart

1. Open **Cart rewards**. **The cart on your store** says whether the app embed
   is on. If it is off, click **Turn on in theme** and save in the theme editor.
2. The cart drawer now shows the progress, the gift and the code field by
   itself.
3. For the cart page, click **Add the block to the cart page**. The theme
   editor opens on the cart template with the **Rewards and code** block ready
   to place; put it where you want it and save.

## Notes

- Without the block, the panel goes to the top of the cart page's summary. The
  block reserves room for the panel before it fills, so the page moves less
  while it loads.
- The panel takes its fonts and colours from your theme.
- The block shows nothing while no reward is set up.
- What the panel shows: [../concepts/cart-rewards.md](../concepts/cart-rewards.md).
