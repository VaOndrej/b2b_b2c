# Show Milestones in the cart

1. Open **Discounts → Milestones → Milestones in the store**. The **Cart drawer**
   row says whether the app embed is on. If it is off, click **Turn on in
   theme** and save in the theme editor.
2. The cart drawer now shows the ladder (a track with a mark per step and the
   sentence about the next one), the gift and the code field by itself.
3. For the cart page, use the button in the **Cart page** row. The theme
   editor opens on the cart template with the **Milestones and code** block
   ready to place; put it where you want it and save. On the cart page the
   ladder lists every step with its reward, the reached ones ticked.

## Notes

- Without the block, the panel goes to the top of the cart page's summary. The
  block reserves room for the panel before it fills, so the page moves less
  while it loads.
- The panel takes its fonts and colours from your theme.
- The block shows nothing while no step is set up.
- What the panel shows: [../concepts/cart-rewards.md](../concepts/cart-rewards.md).
