# Change the texts on the storefront

1. Open **Look** and expand **Storefront texts**.
2. Each row is one text in three languages (Czech, Slovak, English). The grey text in a field is the default.
3. Type your own wording where you want it. Keep the parts in curly braces — `{min}`, `{amount}`, `{price}` —
   exactly as they are; the store fills them in.
4. Click **Save**. The store shows the new texts within a moment.

To go back to a default, empty the field and save. A text can have at most 500 characters.

The "pieces left" text of the sale badge is not editable here: it changes its form with the number.
