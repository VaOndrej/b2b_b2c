# Change the texts on the storefront

1. Open **Translations**. The first table is the store's default language.
2. Each row is one text: where the customer sees it, the default text in grey, and a field for your own.
3. Type your own wording where you want it. Keep the parts in curly braces — `{min}`, `{amount}`, `{price}` —
   exactly as they are; the store fills them in. A text that drops or adds one cannot be saved.
4. Click **Save**. The store shows the new texts within a moment.

To go back to a default, empty the field and save. A text can have at most 500 characters.

## More languages

Under **Add a language** pick one of the languages switched on in Shopify (Settings → Languages) and a new
table appears. The first time, the app asks for permission to read the store's languages.

Free translates the default language and one more; Pro every language of the store. After a move from Pro to
Free the further languages stay saved, and the store shows the default texts in them.

## Export and import (Pro)

**Download CSV** gives one file with every text and a column per language. Translate it in a spreadsheet and
use **Upload CSV**: the app first shows what would change and what it refuses (an unknown text, a missing part
in curly braces, a text that is too long). Nothing is saved until you confirm.

## What is not here

The "pieces left" text of the sale badge changes its form with the number, so it is not editable. A gift's name
comes from the product; translate it in Shopify.
