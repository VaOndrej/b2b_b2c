# More pieces sold than the clearance quota

Shopify tells Won about an order right after checkout, not during it. When the
last pieces of a quota sell in several checkouts at the same moment, a few
pieces can sell at the sale price before Won ends the sale. The sale then shows
"N pc sold past the quota" with the exact number, and its history lists them.

This is expected and cannot be prevented by an app. Keep the quota a little below
the stock you want to clear if that matters.
