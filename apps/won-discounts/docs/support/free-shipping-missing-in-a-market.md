# Free shipping or the gift is missing in a market

Rewards have an amount **per currency**, never converted by exchange rate. A
market whose currency has no amount gets no free shipping or gift, and its
cart shows no progress for it.

**Milestones** shows a note under the reward, for example "No amount in EUR
(Slovakia): the reward is not offered in that market." Enter the amount in that
currency and save.

## Still missing after you entered it

- Check the shipping: free shipping also follows the combination switches in
  Settings; a discount that does not combine with shipping leaves it out. See
  [../tasks/switch-combination-categories.md](../tasks/switch-combination-categories.md).
- A change just saved can take a short moment to reach checkout. See
  [../concepts/saving-and-syncing.md](../concepts/saving-and-syncing.md).
