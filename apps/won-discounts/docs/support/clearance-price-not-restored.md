# A price did not come back after a clearance sale

Won restores a price only if it still holds the value the sale set. If you (or
another app) changed the price, the compare-at price or a price list's fixed
price while the sale ran, Won keeps your change. The sale's **history** then
shows "A price changed outside the app was kept".

**Fix:** set the price you want in Shopify yourself; Won does not touch it again.

If the history says "Restoring the prices failed; it is retried", Shopify did not
answer; Won retries after 1, 5, 15 and then every 60 minutes, and the sale keeps
its sale mark until the prices are back.
