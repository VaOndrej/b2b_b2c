# Upgrade to Pro, and cancel

## Upgrade

1. Go to **Plan**.
2. Choose Pro. Shopify's own confirmation page opens.
3. Approve the recurring charge.

Billing is handled by **Shopify**, on the same invoice as your other apps. Won
Toasts never sees a card number. If the subscription cannot be started you get a
plain "Billing error" instead of a half-upgraded account.

## Cancel

**Plan** → **Cancel Pro (back to Free)**. You can also cancel from your Shopify
admin's app subscriptions. Cancelling is immediate in the app.

## What happens to your settings on Free

Nothing is deleted. Pro-only settings stop being *applied* — the look falls back
to the default, targeting stops narrowing, extra milestones beyond the Free limit
go inactive, and the "Powered by Won" badge returns. Upgrade again and your
configuration is applied as it was.

This is enforced on the server before the storefront ever sees the config, so a
downgraded store cannot keep serving Pro output.

## What Pro actually buys

Scope, never quality: design studio, custom CSS, targeting, advanced anti-spam
caps, unlimited milestones, Insights and experiments, the Pro recipes, and brand
removal. Accessibility, languages, cart toasts and the live preview are on every
plan. Full list:
[reference/plan-limits](../reference/plan-limits.generated.md).
