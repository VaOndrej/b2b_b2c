# Free vs Pro — the idea

Won Toasts has two plans: **Free** and **Pro** ($5/month). The guiding rule is:

> **Pro gates _scope_, never _quality_.**

That means the Free plan is genuinely usable — it keeps **all** cart events,
Undo, every supported language, the live preview, accessibility, and a clean
default look. Free is not a crippled demo.

Pro adds *more* customization and reach: the design studio, custom CSS, advanced
grouping, targeting, unlimited milestones, analytics, experiments, and it removes
the "Powered by Won" badge.
